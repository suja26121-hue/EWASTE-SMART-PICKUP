Smart E-Waste Collection Portal

Chengalpattu District  ·  Sustainable Future Initiative — Smart Cities

Problem Statement

Most households in Chennai and its surrounding districts have no convenient way to dispose of e-waste, so much of it goes to the unregulated informal sector or into household trash, and authorities have no verified area-wise data. Our platform connects citizens with NGO collection partners for doorstep pickup with driver tracking, routes e-waste to authorized facilities, gives each district a portal with verified data, and rewards top contributors every two months.

How It Works
1
Citizen Requests Pickup
Fill in your details, upload a photo, confirm data wipe, and submit. Optionally join a ward batch day to reduce costs.
2
NGO Assigns Driver
An NGO partner reviews the request, assigns a verified driver, and plans the optimal pickup route.
3
Driver Picks Up & Delivers
The driver accepts the job, picks up e-waste at your doorstep, and delivers it to an authorised facility. You can track the driver live.
4
Facility Verifies & Rewards
Facility staff weigh the batch and record verified kg. District authority sees area-wise data and top contributors earn rewards.
